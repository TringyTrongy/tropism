Caption controls

A test sheet for src/scrub.js and src/audio.js. Every dotted number in the caption below is a control: drag it sideways, or focus it and use the keyboard. Serve the project folder over HTTP (for example with tools/serve.mjs); modules do not load from a file URL.

Fig. 1 | A dish of 5,000 particles in 6 species, each feeling its neighbours within rmax = 0.055 of the unit torus. Velocities halve every 40 ms under a force scale of 10.0, outside a repulsive core of β = 0.30. The sonifier listens to synthetic statistics: each species gathers to as much as 8.0-fold its well-mixed density on a slow cycle, moving at about 2.0 rmax s−1, and the self-affinity aAA = +0.45 sets how much of that cycle species A reaches.
